Projects

CelestialAR

Android AR app for learning astronomy

I built CelestialAR to see how far a phone could go as a teaching tool. Point it at a flat surface and the solar system appears in front of you. Tap a planet to see its facts, a short video and its own accent colour, all in a glowing “Holographic Observatory” interface.

Tested with real students: a teacher ran the same solar system lesson in two classes. The class taught with the app averaged 95% on the post-lesson test, against 80% for the class taught the usual way.
  • Built in Unity with AR Foundation and ARCore, running on a Samsung Galaxy A51.
  • Plane detection with an object spawner, with height offsets so models sit on the surface instead of sinking into it.
  • Custom Fresnel glow shader, scrolling starfield, radial planet menu, and a music playlist that pauses when an info panel opens.
  • Planets from Mercury to Neptune, plus the Sun, Moon and Pluto, scaled to be readable in AR.
  • Debugged a black camera feed, a namespace clash that forced Safe Mode, and Gradle and SDK build failures.
